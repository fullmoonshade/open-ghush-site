# Code of Conduct

GhushSite contributors must keep participation respectful, factual, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and evidence without harassment or personal attacks.
- Protect reporters, moderators, and alleged individuals from identification.
- Use synthetic examples instead of real allegations or personal data.
- Treat every submitted report as an unverified allegation, never a finding of guilt.
- Accept technical review and correct mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Doxxing, threats, harassment, discrimination, or sexualized conduct
- Publishing confidential reports, identities, contact details, or credentials
- Using project channels to accuse or investigate named individuals
- Retaliating against reporters, researchers, contributors, or moderators
- Deliberately weakening privacy or security controls

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's security-advisory channel when confidentiality is required. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors to protect the community and affected individuals.
