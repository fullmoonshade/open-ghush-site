# Data Policy

Apache License 2.0 applies to application source code, not to production report data.

This repository must not contain:

- Production or unpublished reports
- Database dumps or moderation exports
- Names, addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers from submissions
- Screenshots or rendered pages containing real allegations
- Investigative evidence or subject-organized dossiers
- Supabase project metadata or credentials

Public deployments remain responsible for the legal basis, privacy notice, retention, moderation, takedown, and disclosure rules governing their own data. Fork operators must use a separate Supabase project.

A future public dataset requires a separate privacy and legal review, irreversible de-identification, a documented data license, and confirmation that deleted or withheld reports cannot be reconstructed. Aggregate statistics are safer than row-level exports but still require review for small-group re-identification.
